Aixle Insights uses two agreements:

- **Individual CLA (iCLA)** — signed by each individual contributor.
- **Corporate CLA (cCLA)** — signed by an authorized representative of a company
  whose employees contribute in the course of their employment.

Acceptance of the iCLA is collected electronically via **CLA Assistant** on each
pull request. You will be prompted to sign the first time you open a PR; signing
is a one-time action per contributor.

## How signing works

Signing the Individual CLA is electronic, as its § 8 provides, and is a one-time step
that covers all your future contributions to this Project. There is nothing to print or
email.

1. Open your pull request as normal.
2. The CLA bot comments on it with the status and the exact sentence to reply with.
3. From the account that authored the commits, post that sentence as a pull request
   comment, word for word:

   > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

4. The bot records your signature and the check turns green. If it does not update,
   comment `recheck`.

Records kept on acceptance: account identifier, name, email address, and timestamp
(per iCLA § 8).

This Agreement applies in addition to the project's open-source license (the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE)) and to the per-commit
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) sign-off. The DCO
certifies the **origin** of your contribution; the CLA grants the project the **rights**
to it. Both are required.

Contributing on behalf of an employer or other organization? Your organization should
execute the [Corporate CLA](#corporate-cla) instead of each employee signing
individually — see its § 7 for how the two interact. To execute it, contact us at
Dualboot Partners, LLC, 5540 Centerview Dr., Ste. 204, #24754, Raleigh, NC 27606.
