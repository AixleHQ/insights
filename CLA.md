# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Status: CLA required (agreement text pending legal sign-off).**
> Contributions to Aixle Insights require a signed Contributor License
> Agreement. This supersedes any earlier DCO-only / "no CLA required"
> statement.
>
> ⚠️ **Draft notice:** the binding iCLA/cCLA text below is still being
> finalized with counsel. Until it lands, the CLA Assistant check runs in
> **advisory (non-blocking)** mode — you can open and merge PRs, but the
> signing prompt already records intent. Once the final text is published,
> the check becomes required.

Aixle Insights uses two agreements:

- **Individual CLA (iCLA)** — signed by each individual contributor.
- **Corporate CLA (cCLA)** — signed by an authorized representative of a company
  whose employees contribute in the course of their employment.

Acceptance is collected electronically via **CLA Assistant** on each pull
request. You will be prompted to sign the first time you open a PR; signing is a
one-time action per contributor.

## How signing works

1. Open a pull request.
2. The CLA Assistant bot comments with a link to sign (if you haven't already).
3. Read and accept the applicable agreement (individual, or corporate if you
   contribute on behalf of your employer).
4. The check turns green and your PR can proceed to review.

Records kept on acceptance: account identifier, name, email, and timestamp
(per iCLA § 8).
